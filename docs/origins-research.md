## Peru — CAC La Prosperidad de Chirinos / Miguel Ramírez

**Facts**

Already on the site:
- 814 farmers; Miguel farming since 1995, co-op member since 2004; SCA Boston 2019; 1,731m; washed; sun-dried.
- VERIFIED: importer sheet `PERU CHIRINOS GEISHA`.

New:
- Miguel was born in 1976 in Chirinos. His earlier varieties were Caturra, Pache and Catimor. He spoke at the Sustainable Harvest café show in Colombia. Moisture 10.4%, defects 2.8%. VERIFIED: importer sheet.
- Co-op founded **1968**, **817 members, 25% women**, about **2,200 ha**, altitude 1,200–1,900m. Women's and youth committees. Funds university places for **10 members' children a year** with the National University of Jaén. VERIFIED: [Sucafina](https://sucafina.com/na/offerings/prosperidad-de-chirinos-fw).
  - Member count conflict: the importer sheet says 814 and Sucafina says 817. Keep 814 (it's the lot document) or drop the number.
- In 2025 the co-op presented a deforestation-free traceability system at FICAFE 2025. INFERRED: [Practical Action](https://latam.practicalaction.org/la-cooperativa-cafetalera-prosperidad-chirinos-presento-en-la-ficafe-2025-innovador-sistema-peruano-de-acreditacion-de-cafe-libre-de-deforestacion/) headline only.
- The co-op has its own roasted brand, **PACHAKUSHI**. INFERRED: YouTube video title, plus the sacks visible in our photo DSC0753.

**Media we hold:** 11 images in `SNOBI/Peru Geisha/Photos/`, 800–1,280px. These are importer field photos: Miguel, seedlings, nursery, picking, landscapes. Good subjects, too small for full-bleed.

## Ethiopia — Tade GG / Tesfaye Bekele Degaga

**Facts**

Already on the site:
- Shakiso; wildfire and reforestation; mill about 20km away with float-sorting; African beds.
- VERIFIED: importer sheet.

New:
- Farm is **221 ha**. Sucafina lists altitude as 1,860–1,950m; the importer says 1,800–2,200m. Sucafina says Tesfaye travelled Central and South America to learn farming and processing. VERIFIED: [Sucafina](https://sucafina.com/na/offerings/tade-gg-rfa-organic-fully-washed) and the Sucafina PDF in Drive.
- Tesfaye founded **Suke Quto** (his other farm) in **2000**, after the fires. He funds a local school (the Suke school). VERIFIED: [Trabocca Suke Quto](https://www.trabocca.com/our-coffees/ethiopia/guji/suke-quto-farm/), [Suke school project](https://www.trabocca.com/about-us/projects/suko-quto-school-project/).
- Started exporting under the Tade GG name in 2009. INFERRED: roaster blogs.
- Unconfirmed (AI-compiled PDF only): "GG = Green Gold", varieties JARC 74110/74112, a semi-forest system. Verify before use.

## Mexico — GRAPOS + Descamex

**Facts**

Already on the site:
- 3,253 members (832 women); founded 2007; El Triunfo; average 3ha; Mountain Water decaf using Pico de Orizaba water.
- VERIFIED: importer sheet.

New:
- Full name **Grupo de Agricultores Positivos S.P.R. de R.L.**, founded **8 Aug 2007** with 90 producers.
- Programs: free seedlings to renew rust-damaged farms, 8 regional warehouses, pays on delivery, women's knitting program, mushroom cultivation.
- VERIFIED: [Mercanta](https://coffeehunter.com/coffee/grapos-organic/) + roaster pages.
- Other sources give different numbers: Mercanta says 2,708 members (772 women), 700–1,600m. Keep the importer's lot figures or drop numbers.

## Colombia — Cooperativa El Bombo (ASOBOMBO)

**Facts** (all new)
- Producer: **Cooperativa de Caficultores El Bombo – Núcleo 2**, Vereda El Bombo, Pitalito, Huila.
- **82 growers, 303.2 ha, 460.88 t dry parchment.** Organic JAS certificate **3-2493-1** by **Mayacert**.
- Exporter **Café Colsuaves S.A.S.**, Pereira (Mayacert JAS 3-1074-5). Importer **双日食料**.
- Lot 3/0104/00023; 600 bags via Buenaventura → Kobe; ICO certificate of origin from FNC; warehoused July 2025.
- VERIFIED: `20250827コロンビアウィラ(有機証明書.pdf` (10 pages).
- Founded by third-generation producer **John Edison Díaz**. About 95% of members farm under 5ha; 85 women members. Members also in Caquetá, Cauca and Nariño.
- Distributed about 100,000 shade trees in Cauca. Helped build panela mills. A youth extension team works on reforestation and water saving.
- VERIFIED: [Sucafina El Bombo](https://sucafina.com/na/offerings/el-bombo-organic-fw).

**Copy implication:** the site says 「ウィラ南部の複数農園（ASOBOMBO生産者協会）」. It can now name the co-op properly. Owner call.
