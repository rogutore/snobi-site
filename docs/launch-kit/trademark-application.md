## 1. 商標登録願 (paper, A4 portrait)
Layout (様式第２ 備考, JPO PDF; that edition still says 平成, so check the layout against the current form):
- top margin at least 6 cm (for the stamps); left, right and bottom 2 cm;
- 36 characters per line, at most 29 lines per page, 10–12 pt, black;
- no half-width characters;
- the 標準文字 mark on one line, in the same type and size, at least 10 pt.

```
　　コーヒー，焙煎したコーヒー豆，コーヒー豆（生のもの），コーヒー飲料，ミルクコーヒー

```

Notes on each field:
- **Field order:** the mark text goes right after 【商標登録を受けようとする商標】, followed by the 【標準文字】 line. VERIFIED, JPO 電子出願 guide 「標準文字のみによって商標登録を受けようとする場合」.
- **Why 「ＳＮＯＢＩ」:** 「ö」 is not an allowed 標準文字 character (`trademark.md`, VERIFIED).
- **Goods:** exactly as in `trademark.md`, separated by 「，」 (様式第２ 備考11ロ). All five goods are listed in the **類似商品・役務審査基準〔国際分類第13-2026版対応〕**, class 30 (VERIFIED, read on 2026-10-09 from `ruiji_kijun13-2026-all.pdf`): コーヒー, コーヒー飲料, 焙煎したコーヒー豆 and ミルクコーヒー under the コーヒー entry (29B01); コーヒー豆（生のもの） under 32D04. **This is the 対象3 condition.** The edition that applies follows the filing year, so a 2026 filing uses the 2026 edition (guideline p.28).
- **【代表者】:** required because the applicant is a company filing without an agent (guideline p.36; 様式第２ 備考17).
- **【整理番号】:** optional. Up to 10 characters of capital letters, digits and 「－」 (備考６). It is reused in the 事情説明書 to identify the application before its number is known.

### The identity risk: read this before filing
- **The rule:** the mark in use must be **identical** to the mark applied for. For early examination, JPO accepts only marks that look almost the same: 「外観上酷似する場合（商標を構成する文字や図形が原則同じ場合）のみ」. Upper and lower case count as identical (「ＪＰＯ／ｊｐｏ」). Romaji and katakana, or a heavily stylised logo, do not (guideline p.18, VERIFIED).
- **The risk:** **「SNöBI」 with ö is a different character from 「Ｏ」**, so the ö form may be judged not identical (INFERRED).
- **What carries the evidence:** pages showing **「Snobi」** in plain letters. That is a case difference only, so it should count as identical.
- **What exists today:** the live site and the new v3 footer both show 「Snobi」.
- **What to do:** capture item 1 **before** this weekend's switch (the live site, VERIFIED to show 「© 2026 Snobi」 today) **and** after it. Keep a plain 「Snobi」 or 「SNOBI」 visibly on the product and buy pages until registration. Do not "clean it up" to SNöBI-only.
- **If early examination is refused:** JPO sends the reason, the application continues under normal examination (6–8 months), and a new request can be made once the requirements are met (guideline pp.5, 7). The application itself is not lost.
