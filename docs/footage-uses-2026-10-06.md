## Read this first: the printed Peru label is wrong
On the Peru 100 g back label, every value from 生豆生産国 down sits one row below its heading: 生豆生産国 → 有機レギュラーコーヒー, 内容量 → ペルー, 賞味期限 → 100g, 保存方法 → 2027/04/20. The barcode reads 0000000000000.
- **VERIFIED:** I checked this myself on clip 7208 at 20.0 s (crop: `docs/img/label-7208-misprint.jpg`). The review found the same at 0.2 s and 12.0 s.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether the bags being sold now carry this label, and whether the labels for the other three coffees have the same fault. Ethiopia is UNKNOWN; for Peru clips 7199 and 7209 it is INFERRED.
- This is a 食品表示 matter for the owner, not something to fix on the website. Until the label is fixed, the site and social use front-label shots only.

## What the footage is
- Everything was filmed on one morning in Tokyo: the roastery bench, the roaster, a café bar and an outdoor brew table. There is no origin or farm footage, so every caption must say Tokyo.
- Seven clips show SNöBI bags: 6586 (four-bag table), 6572 (Ethiopia on the sunlit desk), 7111 (four bags behind a mug pour), 7074 (Mexico decaf beside a pour-over), and 7199, 7208 and 7209 (Peru in hand).
- Clip 7695 is an "ode by TOKYO COFFEE" bag, so it is never used for SNöBI. Clip 7669 is unconfirmed: the bag is upside-down and the name never shows.
- The café clips 6786–6791 have no SNöBI bag and are dominated by other brands (La Marzocco, Torani, OCD, HARIO). They stay off the SNöBI pages.
- Falling beans can't be slowed down: the slow-motion processing leaves ghost streaks, so every bean-pour shot runs at normal speed. Slow motion is only for still or slow subjects: bags on the table, the mug pour, the coffee bloom and the cooling-tray arm.
- Bag shots don't fit a tall phone frame (9:16), so they run as 16:9, 4:5 or 1:1. Only falling columns of beans and textures crop cleanly to tall.
- The only dark material is the roast: the drum, the chute and the cooling tray. That is the site's one dark band.
